# Grok 4.6

Grok 4.6 is a first-party Cursor model, built together with SpaceXAI for coding and other knowledge work. It builds on Grok 4.5 with a focus on longer agent runs and more ambitious interactive and visual work. For most new work, use [Grok 4.7](https://cursor.com/help/models-and-usage/grok-4-7.md).

## What is Grok 4.6?

Grok 4.6 is designed for difficult, long-running tasks that require creative tool use, whether in software engineering, data science, finance, legal work, or anything else you do on a computer. It is especially strong at staying with complex multi-step work and turning a broad product idea into a working first version.

- General model for coding and general knowledge work
- Same base as Grok 4.5, pretrained from scratch
- Responds quickly for short questions; works longer for hard ones
- Strong on ambitious long-running tasks on large codebases
- Strong instruction-following even with many skills/rules
- Safest model yet, with improved cyber and moderation

While Composer remains available for everyday coding speed and cost, Grok 4.6 is the general model aimed at harder, longer sessions.

## What are Grok 4.6 effort levels?

Grok 4.6 supports four effort levels: **low**, **medium**, **high**, and **xhigh**, so you can match compute to how hard the task is. Higher effort lets the model think longer on difficult tasks. The named-model default is **high**. When using Auto, Cursor uses the same default (**high**) at launch.

On Plus and on the legacy [Cursor Start](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/cursor-start.md) plan, Grok 4.6 is fixed at medium effort in non-fast mode. You cannot change the effort level or enable Fast mode on those plans. Move to Premium or higher, or a legacy Pro or higher plan, to choose effort levels and Fast mode.

## When should I choose Grok 4.6 over Composer?

- Choose **Grok 4.6** for harder, longer-running work: multi-step agents, ambitious interactive or visual first passes, large codebases, and tasks that stalled or felt short on Grok 4.5.
- Choose **Composer** for everyday coding where speed and cost matter most. Composer is not being deprecated.

## Which plans include Grok 4.6?

Grok 4.6 uses the same first-party / Composer credit treatment as Grok 4.5. Credits that apply to Grok 4.5 also apply to Grok 4.6. Included plan usage from the first-party pool covers 4.6 the same way.

Every paid plan includes access to Grok 4.6: Plus, Premium, Super, Ultra, Teams, and Enterprise on The X Subscription, and the legacy Start (India), Pro, Pro+, and Ultra plans. Grok 4.6 draws from the usage pool for Cursor models included with your plan. On Plus and on the legacy Start plan, Grok 4.6 runs at a fixed medium effort level in non-fast mode.

## Where can I use Grok 4.6?

Grok 4.6 is available everywhere you use Cursor:

- Desktop app
- Web and cloud agents
- CLI
- SDK
- Automations
- iOS app

## Which regions support Grok 4.6?

Grok 4.6 is available in every country where Cursor normally offers models, including the EU. See [Regions and model availability](https://cursor.com/help/security-and-privacy/regions.md) for what to do if a model is unavailable in your region.

## Is Composer being deprecated?

No. Composer remains available for everyday coding speed and cost; Grok 4.6 is the general model for harder, longer work.

## Why can't I see Grok 4.6?

Enterprise team admins manage model access at [cursor.com/dashboard/team-settings/models](https://cursor.com/dashboard/team-settings/models). See [model and integration management](https://cursor.com/docs/enterprise/model-and-integration-management.md) for details.

If Cursor models or Grok / SpaceXAI models are blocked for your team, an admin must enable them before members can use Grok 4.6.

Grok 4.6 is served under the Cursor vendor catalog, so teams that previously blocked the xAI provider can still enable 4.6.
