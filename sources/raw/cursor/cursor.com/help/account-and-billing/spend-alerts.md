# Spend alerts

Get email notifications when your team's additional usage spend reaches a threshold you set. Spend alerts don't stop usage; use [spend limits](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/spend-limits.md) to cap costs.

## How do I set up spend alerts?

On a legacy Cursor Teams plan:

1. Go to your [team dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard/spending)
2. Click **Add Alert**
3. Choose team-level or member-level
4. Set your spend threshold

When on-demand spend hits the threshold, the relevant members receive an email showing the threshold exceeded and current spend. Admins can choose whether member-level alerts go to admins, the member, or both.

On The X Subscription, spend alerts are available to Teams admins under **Billing** › **Alerts** at [grok.com/dashboard/billing/alerts](https://grok.com/dashboard/billing/alerts), and additional usage must be turned on first. Individual plans don't have spend alerts; set a [monthly additional usage limit](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/spend-limits.md#how-do-i-set-a-monthly-additional-usage-limit-on-the-x-subscription) instead.

## What counts toward spend alert thresholds?

Spend alerts trigger based on additional usage spend only: on-demand spend on legacy plans, or prepaid-credit usage on The X Subscription. Included usage in your plan does not count.

Enterprise accounts with pooled usage can set member-level alerts based on total spend, not just on-demand spend.
