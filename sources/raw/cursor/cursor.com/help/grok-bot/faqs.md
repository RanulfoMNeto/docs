# Grok Bot FAQs

Short answers for access, usage, service status, and using Grok Bot. Longer steps live on the linked articles.

## Do I need a separate Grok Bot subscription?

No. Grok Bot comes with **Premium**, **Super**, and **Ultra** on The X Subscription, and with legacy **Cursor Pro**, **Pro+**, **Ultra**, and self-serve **Teams**. On a legacy Cursor account you can also link an individual **SuperGrok**, **SuperGrok Plus**, **SuperGrok Heavy**, or **X Premium+** account. On Enterprise, ask your account team. See [Plans and billing](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#how-do-i-get-access-to-grok-bot).

## Is Grok Bot the same as the Grok app?

No. Grok (the chat app from xAI) and Grok Bot are two different apps. A paywall or a **Weekly SuperGrok Limit** in the Grok app is not about Grok Bot. Grok Bot signs in with your Cursor account. See [Sign in to Grok Bot](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/sign-in.md).

## Which Grok plans can I link?

Individual **SuperGrok**, **SuperGrok Plus**, **SuperGrok Heavy**, and **X Premium+**. **SuperGrok Lite** and **Grok Enterprise** cannot link. If you have a seat on a self-serve **Grok Business** plan, you don't link from the plan screen. Sign out, then sign back in with **Sign in with Grok** using the Grok account that holds the seat. See [Which SuperGrok tiers grant Grok Bot?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok.md#which-supergrok-tiers-grant-grok-bot).

## I don't see Link Grok Account.

**Link Grok Account** is on the **Get Started with Grok Bot** page at [cursor.com/bot/onboarding](https://cursor.com/bot/onboarding), and on the phone's paywall screen. If you already have access, open **Settings** > **Usage & Billing** and look under **Manage Plan** for **Get Access with Grok**. It appears only when linking would raise your usage. See [Why don't I see a Link Grok Account option after I've already logged in?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok.md#why-dont-i-see-a-link-grok-account-option-after-ive-already-logged-in).

## I linked SuperGrok to the wrong Cursor account. Can I unlink it?

No. A link is permanent, and you cannot move it yourself. [Contact support](https://cursor.com/help.md) with the account that got the link and the account that should have it. See [Can I unlink or move a SuperGrok link?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok.md#can-i-unlink-or-move-a-supergrok-link-to-a-different-cursor-account).

## Can I change the email on my account?

No. The email on a Cursor account cannot change. To use a different email, make a new account. See [Change your email address](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/change-email.md).

## I'm stuck in a sign-in loop or see an authentication error.

Update the app, then sign out and sign in again. Clear cookies for cursor.com and x.ai and try a private window. If the phone loops, sign in on desktop first. If the error is new, wait 30 to 60 minutes. Reinstalling usually does not help. See [Sign in to Grok Bot](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/sign-in.md#why-is-grok-bot-showing-a-persistent-authentication-error-even-after-i-log-out-clear-cache-and-reinstall).

## Is Grok Bot down?

Check [status.cursor.com](https://status.cursor.com) and look at the **Grok Bot** row. The app doesn't show service status, so the status page is the place to check. You can subscribe to updates there.

If the status page shows a Grok Bot incident:

- Wait for the incident to be resolved. The status page posts updates.
- Don't **Reset** the computer or reinstall the app. Neither fixes a service problem, and **Reset** can lose very recent changes if the computer can't be reached to save them.
- When the incident is resolved, send your message again.

If the status page shows no incident and the Bot says **Bot failed to respond** or **Model provider is overloaded**, wait a moment and send the message again. If the Bot still doesn't reply, fully quit and reopen Grok Bot, then follow [How do I troubleshoot Grok Bot computer, routine, connector, and startup problems?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/how-to.md#how-do-i-troubleshoot-grok-bot-computer-routine-connector-and-startup-problems) If it keeps happening, [contact support](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/get-help.md) with the request ID.

## My Bot stopped replying and says I hit my usage limit. Is it broken?

No. Your weekly usage is used up. It resets each week. To keep going now, add prepaid credit (The X Subscription), turn on on-demand usage (legacy Cursor plans), or move to a bigger plan. See [How does Grok Bot usage work?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#how-does-grok-bot-usage-work).

## Why doesn't my SuperGrok limit match my Grok Bot usage?

They are two separate meters for two products. Grok Bot usage lives on your Cursor account. See it in Grok Bot under **Settings** > **Usage & Billing**, not in the Grok app. See [What do the plan and spending screens call usage?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#what-do-the-plan-and-spending-screens-call-usage).

## Do a Cursor plan and SuperGrok add together?

No. On a legacy Cursor account, Grok Bot uses whichever one grants more weekly usage. Legacy **Pro**, **SuperGrok**, and **X Premium+** are the same tier, **Pro+** matches **SuperGrok Plus**, and **Ultra** matches **SuperGrok Heavy**. For more usage, move to a higher tier or turn on on-demand usage. On The X Subscription there's nothing to link: Grok Bot draws from your plan's shared weekly pool. See [Do Cursor and SuperGrok plans stack?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#do-cursor-and-supergrok-plans-stack).

## How do I stop charges after my weekly usage runs out?

On The X Subscription, leave Auto Top-up off or set a monthly additional usage limit under **Additional usage credits** at [grok.com/dashboard/billing](https://grok.com/dashboard/billing). On a legacy Cursor plan, turn off on-demand or set a cap: in Grok Bot, open **Settings** > **Usage & Billing** > **On-demand monthly limit**, or on the web go to [cursor.com/dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard) > **Spending** > **Monthly Limit**. The cap does not stop a Bot in the middle of a run, so a running task can go a little past it. See [How does the on-demand monthly limit apply to Grok Bot?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#how-does-the-on-demand-monthly-limit-apply-to-grok-bot) and [Usage-based charges](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/overages.md).

## My trial ran out in one task. Can I get it back?

No. The trial is a usage credit, and one big task can use all of it. Used credit is not restored. See [What if my trial ran out during a single run?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#what-if-my-trial-ran-out-during-a-single-run).

## I paid in the iPhone or Android app. How do I cancel or get a refund?

Apple or Google handles it. Cancel in **Apple ID** > **Subscriptions**, or in **Google Play** > **Payments & subscriptions**. Cursor cannot cancel or refund store purchases. See [Subscribe from the iOS app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/app-store-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription), [Subscribe from the Android app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/google-play-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription), and [Refunds](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/refunds.md).

## Do I have to pay again on my phone?

No. Sign in to the phone app with the same Cursor account, and your access carries over. See [Do I need to buy again on mobile?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/mobile.md#do-i-need-to-buy-again-on-mobile-if-my-cursor-account-already-includes-grok-bot).

## Does my Bot keep working when my laptop is closed?

Yes. Bots work on a cloud computer. Closing the app, the laptop, or the phone does not stop a task or a routine. See [Routines](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/routines.md).

## Does the Bot use my own computer?

Bots work on their own cloud computer. A Bot runs a command on your desktop only if you allow it. The setting is under **Settings** > **Computer** > **Your computer**, labeled **Execution on this computer**. If no desktop is listed there yet, the same page shows **Execution on Local Computer**. By default it asks you each time. See [Grok Bot settings](https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/settings.md).

## Why does a website block my Bot or show a captcha?

Some sites block traffic from cloud computers. When that happens, the Bot hands you the step, and you can sign in yourself on the Bot's computer. You can also turn on **Route traffic through this computer** in **Settings** > **Computer**, under **Your computer**, so sites see your own connection.

## My chats or Bots are missing.

They are usually not deleted. Fully quit and reopen. Check another device on the same account. Open **Hidden Bots**. Make sure you are on the same Cursor account. Don't use **Recover** or **Reset** to look for them. See [My chats or bots look missing](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/computer-recovery.md#my-chats-or-bots-look-missing-were-they-deleted).

## The computer says Reconnecting or "Couldn't Reach Grok Bot's Computer."

Wait and choose **Retry**. Then fully quit and reopen. Then **Update**, then **Recover**. Use **Reset** only last. Like **Recover**, it can lose very recent changes if the computer can't be reached to save them. See [Which should I try first?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/computer-recovery.md#which-should-i-try-first).

## What does the low disk space warning mean?

**Computer is low on disk space** or **Computer is critically low on disk space** is about your Bots' cloud computer, not your laptop. Choose **Go to Disk Saver**. Disk Saver is a Bot that checks what is using space and suggests what to clean up. It asks before it deletes anything, and nothing is deleted unless you confirm. Your chats are not affected. See [My chats or bots look missing](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/computer-recovery.md#my-chats-or-bots-look-missing-were-they-deleted).

## Why did an approval expire before I saw it?

Approvals from work that started without you, like a routine or a message from another Bot, expire after about 10 minutes. Approvals in a chat you are in wait for you. Turn on the Bot's **Notifications**, or choose **Always allow** for actions you trust. See [Why did an approval expire?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/how-to.md#why-did-an-approval-expire)

## Can I pick the model?

No. Grok Bot picks the model for each task, and there is no model picker. See [Which AI models power Grok Bot?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/models.md).

## Which devices work?

The desktop app runs on Mac, Windows, and Linux. The phone app runs on iPhone and Android. Get the desktop app from the Grok Bot page at [cursor.com/dashboard/bot](https://cursor.com/dashboard/bot). See [Grok Bot on mobile](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/mobile.md).

## How do I contact support?

[Contact support](https://cursor.com/help.md) while signed in to your Cursor account. Include your account email, your Grok Bot version (account menu > **Support** > **About** > **Copy version info**), a screenshot, and the request ID (right-click the message > **Copy request ID**). See [How do I find my Grok Bot version?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/how-to.md#how-do-i-find-my-grok-bot-version) and [How do I copy a request ID?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/how-to.md#how-do-i-copy-a-request-id).
